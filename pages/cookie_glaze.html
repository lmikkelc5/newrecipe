Cookie Glaze

Ingredients

  • 2 cups powdered sugar
  • 1 Tbsp milk
  • 6 Tbsp light corn syrup
  • Food coloring

Directions

  1. Mix sugar, milk, corn syrup.
  2. Add more corn syrup, a Tbsp at a time, to thin if needed.
  3. Tint with coloring.
  4. If sprinkles are used, add immediately.

This is a flow frosting, it should be fairly thick consistency but still spreadable. Frosting will flow slightly for a smooth look.
