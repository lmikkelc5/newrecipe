Scalloped Potatoes

Ingredients

  • 2 lb potatoes (6 medium)
  • 3 Tbsp margarine or butter
  • 3 Tbsp flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp pepper
  • 2 ½ cups milk
  • 1 small onion, chopped (¼ cup)
  • 1 Tbsp margarine or butter

Directions

  1. Cut potatoes into thin slices to measure 4 cups.
  2. Melt 3 Tbsp butter in sauce pan over low heat; stir in flour, salt and pepper. Cook over low heat, stirring constantly, until mixture is smooth and bubbly. Remove from heat.
  3. Stir in milk. Heat till boiling, stirring constantly. Boil and stir for one minute.
  4. Arrange potatoes in greased 2 qt casserole pan in 3 layers, topping each of the first 2 with ½ onion and ⅓ of white sauce.
  5. Top with remaining potatoes and sauce.
  6. Dot with 1 Tbsp butter, cover and bake at 325°F for 40 minutes or at 350°F for 30 minutes.
  7. Uncover and bake until potatoes are tender, 60-70 minutes.
  8. Let stand 5-10 minutes before serving.
