Chicken Gnocchi Soup

Ingredients

  • Chicken breasts
  • Olive oil
  • Italian seasoning
  • Lemon pepper
  • Smoked paprika
  • Garlic powder
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Butter
  • Garlic
  • Carrots
  • Yellow onion
  • Celery
  • Chicken broth
  • Half and half
  • Flour
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Parsley
  • Basil
  • Gnocchi
  • Spinach
  • Red pepper flakes (optional)
  • Nutmeg (optional)

Directions

  1. In a bowl, combine the chicken with olive oil, Italian seasoning, lemon pepper, smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper. Mix well to coat.
  2. Heat an oiled skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and cook until golden brown, about 4–5 minutes per side. Lower the heat and cook until the internal temperature reaches 165°F. Remove and let rest for 10 minutes, then cube or shred.
  3. In a large pot, melt butter with a little olive oil. Sauté garlic, carrots, onion, and celery until the onion is softened.
  4. Add the chicken broth to the pot. In a separate bowl, whisk the half and half with flour, then add it to the soup to thicken.
  5. Season with black pepper, Italian seasoning, sweet paprika, nutmeg, red pepper flakes, and salt. Cover and simmer for 30 minutes.
  6. Stir in the parmesan, parsley, basil, gnocchi, and cooked chicken. Simmer uncovered for about 9 minutes, or until the gnocchi is soft.
  7. Add the spinach, adjust seasoning to taste, remove from heat, and serve warm.

For a lighter version, substitute milk for the half and half. For a richer soup, use heavy cream instead.
