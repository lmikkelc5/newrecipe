Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients

  • 2 ¼ cups flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 cup softened butter or margarine
  • ¼ cup sugar
  • ¾ cup packed brown sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 package (4 serving size) chocolate or vanilla pudding
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 package (12 oz) chocolate chips

Directions

  1. Mix flour with baking soda.
  2. Combine butter, the sugars, vanilla and pudding in a larger bowl. Beat until smooth and creamy.
  3. Beat in eggs.
  4. Gradually add flour mixture.
  5. Stir in chocolate chips (batter will be stiff).
  6. Drop by rounded measuring spoon.
  7. Bake at 375°F for 8-10 minutes.

Makes about 7 dozen.
