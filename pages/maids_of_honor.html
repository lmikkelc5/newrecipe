Maids of Honor

Needs work: No directions for the lemon filling, and no frosting recipe.

Ingredients

Lemon Filling

  • 6 tsp Cream of Wheat
  • 6 tsp butter
  • 6 tsp sugar
  • 12 tsp lemon curd
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 ½ tsp baking powder

Lemon Curd

  • ¼ lb butter
  • Juice of 2 lemons
  • 1 Tbsp cold water
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 cups sugar

Mom Lambert’s Pastry

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ⅔ cup + 1 Tbsp shortening
  • Water (usually ¼ cup or less)

Assembly

  • Jam (I like apricot best)
  • Frosting

Directions

Lemon Curd

  1. Beat and cook over double boiler until thick.

Mom Lambert’s Pastry

  1. Mix together flour and salt.
  2. Take out ⅓ cup flour… Mix with enough water to make a smooth paste (usually ¼ cup or less).
  3. Cut shortening into remaining flour, then add paste. Mix well.
  4. Divide into 2 parts and roll as for pie crust.

Maids of Honor

  1. Cut pastry to fit muffin pans (mini size). (Mom Lambert’s pastry is best.)
  2. Drop ¼ tsp jam in bottom of each crust.
  3. Spoon in Maid of Honor lemon filling.
  4. Bake 10 min. at 400°F.
  5. Frost when cool.
