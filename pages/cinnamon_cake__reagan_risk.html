Cinnamon Cake (Reagan Risk)

Ingredients

Cake

  • 1 ¼ cups flour
  • 1 Tbsp baking powder
  • ¼ cup sugar
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ cup butter
  • 1 egg
  • ⅔ cup milk (dairy or dairy free)
  • 1-1 ½ tsp vanilla
  • Shredded apples (optional)*

Topping

  • 4 Tbsp flour
  • 4 Tbsp butter
  • 6 Tbsp sugar
  • 1 ½ Tbsp cinnamon

Directions

  1. Mix flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt.
  2. Cut in butter.
  3. Add egg, milk, and vanilla. Mix cake until blended.
  4. Pour in a greased pan (8x8 or whatever you like).
  5. Sprinkle on topping - cover the whole top of the cake.
  6. Bake at 400°F for 25 minutes.

*I love to add shredded apples (makes it super moist and tastes like an apple pie).
