The Best Bean and Ham Soup (allrecipes.com Benevolent Empress)

Needs work: The step for combining the ingredients is missing.

Ingredients

  • 1 (20 oz) package 15 bean mixture, soaked overnight
  • 1 ham bone
  • 2 ½ cups cubed ham
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 3 stalks celery, chopped
  • 5 large carrots, chopped
  • 1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes, with liquid
  • 1 (12 fluid ounce) can low-sodium vegetable juice
  • 3 cups vegetable broth
  • 2 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 Tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 Tbsp chili powder
  • 3 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp ground black pepper
  • 1 Tbsp dried parsley
  • 3 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 7 cups low fat, low sodium chicken broth
  • 1 tsp kosher salt

Directions

  1. Simmer over low heat, stirring occasionally, for about 8 hours. Add more chicken broth as needed throughout the day.
  2. Remove the ham bone and season with salt if needed.
  3. Continue to simmer for a couple more hours.
  4. Remove bay leaves before serving.
