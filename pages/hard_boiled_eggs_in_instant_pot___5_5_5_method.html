Hard Boiled Eggs in Instant Pot – 5/5/5 Method

Ingredients

  • Eggs (1 to 16)
  • 1 cup cold water (1.5 cups for an 8-qt Instant Pot)

Directions

  1. Place 1 cup of COLD water in the inner pot of Instant Pot for a 6-qt Instant Pot and 1.5 cups COLD water for an 8-qt Instant Pot.
  2. Add in egg or steam rack and carefully set as many eggs as desired on or in the rack.
  3. Place lid on the pressure cooker and set cook time for 5 minutes on high pressure.
  4. Once cook time has elapsed, let the pressure release for 5 minutes.
  5. Then do a quick release and carefully remove eggs from Instant Pot and place in ice water bath.
  6. Let eggs sit in water bath for 5 minutes.
  7. Remove eggs from the water bath.
  8. Store in the fridge for up to 7 days unpeeled or peel and use within 3 days.

Equipment needed: Electric pressure cooker (6 or 8 qt), egg rack.

You can use as few as 1 egg and up to 16 eggs in a 6-qt pressure cooker.

I find it is best to peel eggs right from the water bath, the shell comes off beautifully every single time.

To help prevent your eggs from cracking during cooking, let your eggs come to room temperature before pressure cooking.

Be sure to use cold water, warm water or hot water will change the cooking time.
