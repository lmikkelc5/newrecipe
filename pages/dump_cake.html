Dump Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 (20 oz) cans cherry pie filling
  • 1 yellow cake mix
  • 1 (12 oz) can lemon-lime soda pop

Directions

  1. Line a 12” Dutch oven with foil.
  2. Pour in pie filling.
  3. Sprinkle cake mix evenly over filling.
  4. Pour pop over the cake mix. Stir gently to combine cake mix and pop only (not pie filling).
  5. Bake for 35 minutes until the top is brown and springs back to the touch. Use approx. 9 coals on the bottom and 10-12 on the top.
