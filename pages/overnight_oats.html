Overnight Oats

Ingredients

  • ½ cup oats
  • 1 Tbsp chia seeds
  • ~2 Tbsp natural peanut butter
  • ~1 Tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 handful of fruit (frozen or fresh)
  • ½ to ⅔ cup almond milk

Directions

  1. Put oats, chia seeds, peanut butter, brown sugar, and fruit in a container.
  2. Add almond milk and stir well.
  3. Place in fridge overnight and enjoy the next morning!

I use Talenti gelato containers (about 10.5 oz).

Can sit in the fridge for up to 3 days.
