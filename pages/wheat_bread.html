Wheat Bread

Ingredients

  • 5 ½ cups hot water
  • ⅓ cup oil
  • ⅔ cup honey
  • 3 ½ Tbsp lemon juice
  • ½ cup white flour
  • 1 Tbsp salt
  • 14-16 cups fresh ground whole wheat
  • 2 ½ Tbsp yeast

Directions

  1. Knead with mixer for 7 minutes.
  2. Remove dough. Roll out on oiled countertop.
  3. Cut into 4-5 pieces, depending on pan size. Place in greased pans.
  4. Let rise 30 minutes, until 1 ¼" above center of pan.
  5. Bake at 350°F, 25 min.
