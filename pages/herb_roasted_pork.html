Herb Roasted Pork

Ingredients

  • 1 tsp rubbed sage
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp pepper
  • 1 clove garlic, crushed
  • 1 (5 lb) boneless pork loin
  • ½ cup sugar
  • 1 TBSP cornstarch
  • ¼ cup vinegar
  • ¼ cup water
  • 2 TBSP soy sauce

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 325°F.
  2. In a bowl, combine sage, salt, pepper, and garlic.
  3. Rub mixture thoroughly over the pork.
  4. Optional: Sear the roast after rubbing with spices and before putting in the oven.
  5. Place pork in an uncovered roasting pan on the middle oven rack.
  6. Bake for approximately 3 hours, or until internal temperature reaches at least 145°F (or preferred doneness).
  7. Meanwhile, combine sugar, cornstarch, vinegar, water, and soy sauce in a small saucepan.
  8. Heat, stirring occasionally, until mixture begins to bubble and thicken slightly.
  9. Brush roast with glaze 3–4 times during the last 30 minutes of cooking.
  10. Pour remaining glaze over roast and serve.
  11. Note: Other pork recipes call for higher internal temperatures (around 180°F); personal preference may vary.
