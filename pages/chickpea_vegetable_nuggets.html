Chickpea Vegetable Nuggets

Needs work: The step for mixing and forming the nuggets is missing.

Ingredients

  • 15 oz chickpeas, cooked and drained
  • ½ cup grated zucchini
  • ½ cup grated carrot
  • ¼ cup minced onion
  • ½ Tbsp Italian seasoning
  • 1 tsp sea salt
  • 1-2 cloves garlic to taste, or sub ½ tsp garlic powder
  • Black pepper to taste
  • ¼ tsp paprika
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tsp olive or avocado oil, plus extra for cooking
  • ½ cup oat flour or your favorite breadcrumbs

Directions

  1. Place shredded zucchini and carrots in the middle of a clean dish towel or large piece of paper towel. Wrap them up and squeeze a few times to help drain out some of the moisture.
  2. Heat cooking oil in a large pan over medium high heat.
  3. Using a heaping tablespoon or a small cookie scoop, portion out the mixture into the pan.
  4. Cook nuggets 3-5 minutes and flip, using the back of your spatula to gently flatten them a bit. Cook for an additional 3-5 minutes or until outside of the nugget has a slightly golden color.
  5. Enjoy warm and with your favorite dipping sauce.

Allow leftovers to cool completely and store in an air-tight container in fridge for 4-5 days.
