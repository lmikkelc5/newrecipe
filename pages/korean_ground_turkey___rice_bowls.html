Korean Ground Turkey & Rice Bowls

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground turkey
  • 4 cloves garlic, grated or minced
  • ¼ cup honey
  • ¼ cup soy sauce
  • 2 tsp sesame oil
  • ¼ tsp ground ginger
  • ¼ tsp crushed red pepper flakes (fairly spicy; use less for milder taste)
  • ¼ tsp ground black pepper
  • Green onions and sesame seeds, for garnish
  • Cooked rice, for serving

Directions

  1. In a large skillet over medium heat, brown the ground turkey for 8–10 minutes until fully cooked.
  2. While the turkey cooks, make the sauce by mixing garlic, honey, soy sauce, sesame oil, ginger, red pepper flakes, and black pepper in a bowl.
  3. Add the sauce to the cooked turkey and cook for 3–4 minutes until the sauce thickens and absorbs into the meat.
  4. Serve the turkey over rice and garnish with green onions and sesame seeds.
