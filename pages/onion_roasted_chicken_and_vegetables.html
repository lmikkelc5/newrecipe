Onion-Roasted Chicken and Vegetables

Ingredients

  • 1 envelope Lipton Recipe Secrets Onion Soup Mix
  • ¼ cup olive or vegetable oil
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • 4 bone-in chicken breast halves (about 2 lbs)
  • 1 lb all-purpose potatoes, cut into small chunks
  • 2 carrots, sliced

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. Mix onion soup mix, oil, and garlic powder.
  3. Place chicken and vegetables in a baking dish.
  4. Pour soup mixture over chicken and vegetables and toss to coat.
  5. Bake uncovered for about 40–45 minutes, or until chicken is cooked through and vegetables are tender.
  6. Makes 4 servings.
