Perfect Pie Crust

Ingredients

  • All Butter Crust
  • 2½ cups all-purpose flour, plus extra for rolling
  • 1 cup (8 oz) unsalted butter, very cold, cut into ½-inch cubes
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 6–8 tablespoons ice water
  • Butter and Shortening Crust
  • 2½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • ¾ cup (12 tablespoons) unsalted butter, chilled, cut into ¼-inch cubes
  • ½ cup vegetable shortening
  • 8 tablespoons ice water
  • Egg Wash
  • 1 tablespoon heavy cream, half-and-half, or milk
  • 1 large egg yolk

Directions

  1. Slowly add ice water, starting with 4 tablespoons, pulsing until the dough just begins to hold together. Add more water a tablespoon at a time if needed.
  2. The dough is ready when a small handful holds together when pinched.
  3. Divide dough into two equal portions and gently form into discs. Do not over-knead.
  4. Wrap each disc in plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 1 hour or up to 2 days.
  5. Remove dough from refrigerator and let sit at room temperature for 5–10 minutes before rolling.
  6. Roll dough on a lightly floured surface to a 12-inch circle, about ⅛-inch thick.
  7. Place dough into a 9-inch pie dish and gently press into the bottom and sides.
  8. Trim dough to within ½ inch of the edge of the dish.
  9. If making a double crust, roll out second disc and place over filling. Trim and crimp edges.
  10. Flute edges or press with a fork.
  11. Cut vents in the top crust to allow steam to escape.
  12. Pre-Baking a Pie Crust
  13. For a pre-baked crust, prepare a single bottom crust with no filling.
  14. Freeze crust for at least 30 minutes.
  15. Preheat oven to 350°F.
  16. Line crust with foil and fill with pie weights, dry beans, rice, or sugar.
  17. Bake at 350°F for 45–50 minutes if crust will be baked again, or 60–75 minutes if fully baked.
  18. Cool completely before filling.
  19. Egg Wash
  20. Beat egg yolk with cream and brush over crust before baking for a glossy finish.
