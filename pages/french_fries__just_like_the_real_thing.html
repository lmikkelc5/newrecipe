French Fries (just like the real thing?)

Ingredients

  • 2 lb large russet potatoes
  • Safflower or vegetable oil
  • Coarse sea salt

Directions

  1. Peel potatoes; cut into long french-fry sticks, about ⅜x⅜ inches thick. Rinse; shake off water.
  2. Transfer potatoes to a large deep heavy pot, spreading potatoes so they're no more than 2 layers deep.
  3. Pour in oil to cover potatoes by 1". Place the pot over medium heat. Cook for 15 minutes (oil will begin bubbling gently).
  4. Continue cooking, occasionally loosening potatoes from the bottom of the pot with a heatproof spatula, until potatoes are very tender, 25-30 minutes more.
  5. Increase heat to medium-high and cook until golden and crisp, about 15 minutes longer (oil will bubble more vigorously).
  6. Using a slotted spoon, transfer fries to paper towels to drain. Season with coarse sea salt.
