Italian Roasted Vegetables

Ingredients

  • 1 lb unpeeled red potatoes (3-4 medium)
  • 2 large carrots
  • 1 large yellow summer squash
  • 1 large zucchini
  • 8 oz large mushrooms
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, pressed
  • 2 tsp Italian seasoning*
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp ground black pepper

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F.
  2. Cut potatoes in half lengthwise using a Crinkle cutter; cut each half lengthwise into 1-inch-wide wedges. Cut carrots diagonally into 2-inch pieces. Cut mushrooms in half.
  3. Place vegetables in a large bowl; toss with oil.
  4. Press garlic over vegetables using a garlic press. Sprinkle with Italian seasoning, salt and black pepper; toss to coat evenly.
  5. Spread vegetables in a single layer on a stoneware bar pan.
  6. Bake for 40-45 minutes or until golden brown and crisp-tender, stirring after 20 minutes.

*Pantry Italian Seasoning Mix can be substituted for the Italian seasoning.

Makes 6 servings.

Nutrients per serving: Calories 170, Total Fat 9 g, Saturated fat 1.5 g, Cholesterol 0 mg, Carbohydrate 21 g, Protein 4 g, Sodium 4 g, Sodium 210 mg, Fiber 3 g.
