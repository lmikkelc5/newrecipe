Candy Cane Drink

Ingredients

  • 5 to 6 drops peppermint extract
  • ½ cup milk
  • 1 cup vanilla ice cream
  • Grenadine
  • Aerosol whipped cream
  • Large candy cane

Directions

  1. In a blender combine peppermint extract, milk and vanilla ice cream. Blend until smooth.
  2. Swirl with grenadine from bottom to top.
  3. Quickly pour in the blended mixture.
  4. Top with aerosol whipped cream and the candy cane.

Makes one serving.
