Crispy Bang Bang Salmon Bites

Ingredients

For the Salmon Bites

  • 1 ½ lb salmon fillets (skin removed, cut into bite-sized pieces)
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ¼ tsp cayenne pepper (optional)

For the Bang Bang Sauce

  • ½ cup mayonnaise
  • ¼ cup Thai sweet chili sauce
  • 1 Tbsp sriracha (adjust to taste)
  • 1 Tbsp honey
  • 1 Tbsp lime juice (fresh)

Optional Garnishes

  • Fresh parsley or cilantro, chopped
  • Green onions, sliced
  • Sesame seeds

Directions

Prep the Salmon

  1. Pat salmon dry with paper towels. Remove skin and cut into 1-inch cubes.
  2. Place salmon in a bowl. Add olive oil, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, salt, pepper, and cayenne (optional).
  3. Toss gently to coat evenly.

Make the Bang Bang Sauce

  1. Whisk together mayonnaise, Thai sweet chili sauce, sriracha, honey, and lime juice until smooth.
  2. Taste and adjust (more sriracha for heat, more honey for sweetness). Set aside.

Cook the Salmon

  1. Heat a non-stick skillet over medium-high heat with a small amount of oil.
  2. Add salmon in a single layer (work in batches to avoid crowding).
  3. Sear 2–3 minutes per side, until golden and cooked through (tender and flaky inside).
  4. Transfer to a paper towel–lined plate to absorb excess oil.

Coat and Serve

  1. Either drizzle sauce over the salmon bites, or toss salmon gently in a bowl with the sauce.
  2. Garnish with herbs, green onions, or sesame seeds and serve immediately.

Tips for maximum crispiness:

Pat the salmon dry: Less moisture = more crisping, less steaming.

Optional coating: Lightly dust with cornstarch, or coat with panko for extra crunch.

Don’t overcrowd: Cook in batches so the salmon sears instead of steaming.

Hot oil matters: If pan-searing, make sure the oil is hot before adding salmon (aim ~375°F/190°C if measuring).
