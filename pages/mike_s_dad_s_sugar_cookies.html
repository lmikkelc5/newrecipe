Mike’s Dad’s Sugar Cookies

Ingredients

  • 1 cup butter
  • 1 ½ cups sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • ¼ cup milk
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 4 tsp baking powder
  • 5 cups flour
  • 1 Tbsp extract - vanilla, lemon, or cherry, etc.

Directions

  1. Cream butter and sugar together.
  2. Mix in the eggs.
  3. Alternately sift in milk, salt, baking powder, flour and extract.
  4. Bake at 350°F for 12 mins?
