French Lentil Rice Soup

Ingredients

  • 6 cups chicken broth or vegetable broth
  • 1 cup lentils, picked over and rinsed
  • 2 medium carrots, peeled and finely diced
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 2 celery stalks, finely diced
  • 3 Tbsp uncooked white rice
  • 2 Tbsp minced garlic
  • 1 tsp herbes de Provence or dried thyme
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ⅛ tsp ground white or black pepper
  • ¼ cup heavy cream or sour cream (optional)
  • ¼ cup chopped parsley (optional)

Directions

  1. Stir together broth, lentils, carrots, onion, celery, rice, garlic, seasoning, salt and pepper in a slow cooker.
  2. Cover and cook on low for 8 hours or on high for 4 to 5 hours.
  3. Divide soup evenly among 4 serving bowls, garnishing each with cream and parsley if desired.

Note: This is the original recipe. I found when I made it that the thyme flavor was too strong. I used 1 ½ times the recipe to make it for my family and I only use ½-¾ tsp thyme for that amount. I also usually skip the step that calls for pureeing the soup.
