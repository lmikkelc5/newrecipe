Mexican Dip

Ingredients

  • 2 cans bean dip or refried beans
  • 1 small sour cream
  • ½ package taco seasoning
  • 2 avocados (peeled and mashed)
  • 1-2 tsp lemon juice
  • Sprinkle of garlic salt
  • Sharp grated cheese
  • 2 chopped tomatoes
  • Chopped green onions
  • Chopped black olives
  • Tortilla chips

Directions

  1. Mix sour cream with taco seasoning.
  2. Mix mashed avocados with lemon juice and garlic salt.
  3. Layer bean dip, sour cream mixture, and avocado mixture.
  4. Cover with grated cheese, then top with tomatoes, green onions, and black olives.
  5. Serve with tortilla chips!
