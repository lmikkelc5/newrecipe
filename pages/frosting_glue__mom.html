Frosting Glue (Mom)

Ingredients

  • 2 egg whites
  • 2 ¾ (2-3) cups powdered sugar
  • Water, as needed

Directions

  1. Beat egg whites until soft peaks form.
  2. Gradually add powdered sugar and beat for 3 minutes.
  3. Add a drizzle of water as needed.

For gingerbread houses.

Refrigerate leftovers in a tightly covered jar.
