Triple Chocolate Bundt Cake

Ingredients

Cake

  • 1 (18.25 oz) cake mix - German Chocolate with pudding in mix, or other chocolate cake mix
  • ¾ cup water
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 cup butter or margarine, softened
  • 1 (4 oz) pkg. instant chocolate pudding and pie filling
  • 1 cup mini semi-sweet chocolate chips

Glaze

  • ½ cup chocolate chips
  • 2 Tbsp butter
  • 1 Tbsp light corn syrup
  • ¼ tsp vanilla

Directions

Cake

  1. Preheat oven to 325°F. Grease and flour a 12 cups bundt pan.
  2. In a large bowl combine cake mix, water, eggs, butter and pudding. Beat on low until moistened.
  3. Beat 2 minutes on high until well incorporated.
  4. Fold in chocolate chips.
  5. Spoon into the greased and floured bundt pan.
  6. Bake for 1 hour or until edges begin to pull away from the pan.
  7. Cool for 15 minutes. Invert cake onto serving plate.

Glaze

  1. In a saucepan, heat chocolate chips, butter, corn syrup, and vanilla over low heat until melted and smooth.
