Parmesan Crusted Chicken

Ingredients

Breading

  • ½ cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 eggs
  • ⅔ cup seasoned Italian bread crumbs
  • ⅓ cup freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • 2 Tbsp finely chopped fresh herbs (thyme, parsley, chives, or rosemary)

Chicken

  • 3–4 boneless skinless chicken breasts (about 1 ½ lb), pounded ¼-inch thick
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper
  • 2 Tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • Lemon wedges, for serving

Directions

  1. Set up three shallow bowls: flour in the first, beaten eggs in the second, and bread crumbs mixed with Parmesan, salt, pepper, and herbs in the third.
  2. Season chicken breasts with salt and pepper.
  3. Dredge chicken lightly in flour, shaking off excess.
  4. Dip chicken in egg mixture, allowing excess to drip off.
  5. Dredge chicken in bread crumb mixture until well coated.
  6. Heat vegetable oil and olive oil in a large sauté pan over medium heat.
  7. Add chicken and cook 2–3 minutes per side, until golden brown and cooked through.
  8. Serve immediately with lemon wedges.

Note: If chicken breasts are large, they can be cut in half after pounding.
