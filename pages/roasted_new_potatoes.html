Roasted New Potatoes

Ingredients

  • New potatoes
  • Butter
  • Seasonings: salt, rosemary, whatever
  • Parmesan

Directions

  1. Cut potatoes in ½.
  2. Melt butter in bottom of Pyrex. Put potatoes in.
  3. Add seasonings.
  4. Microwave until soft, or roast in the oven at 400°F for 20-30 minutes, not covered.
  5. Sprinkle with parmesan at the end and then cover.
