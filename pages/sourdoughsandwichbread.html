Sourdough Sandwich Bread

Ingredients

  • ½ cup butter, softened
  • 2 Tbsp honey (or sugar)
  • 1 Tbsp salt
  • 1 cup starter, active and bubbly
  • 2 ½ cups water
  • 8 cups flour

Directions

  1. Combine all ingredients and knead until combined, roughly 10 min in a stand mixer. It should pull away from the sides and be stretchy and smooth.
  2. Bulk rise for 10-12 hrs.
  3. Divide it in 2 equal parts and shape by rolling into a rectangle and then rolling it up.
  4. Place in buttered bread pans.
  5. Leave to rise for 2-4 more hours; it should double in size.
  6. Optional: egg wash if you want more browning.
  7. Bake at 375°F for 45 min, or until golden brown on top.
  8. Allow it to cool completely before slicing.
