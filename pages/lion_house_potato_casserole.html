Lion House Potato Casserole

Ingredients

  • 5 large potatoes
  • 3 Tbsp butter/margarine, melted
  • 1 can (10 ½ oz) cream of chicken soup
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 1 cup milk
  • 3 Tbsp finely chopped green onions
  • ¾ cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • ¾ cup corn flake crumbs mixed with 2 Tbsp melted butter
  • 3 Tbsp parmesan cheese

Directions

  1. Boil unpared potatoes until tender. Drain and peel.
  2. Shred coarsely. Place in 2-3 qt casserole pan.
  3. Pour melted butter over potatoes.
  4. Mix together soup, sour cream, milk, onions, and cheese. Pour over potatoes. Do not mix.
  5. Cover top with buttered crumbs mixed with parmesan cheese.
  6. Bake for 30 minutes at 325°F.
