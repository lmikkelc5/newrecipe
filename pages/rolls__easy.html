Rolls (Easy!)

Ingredients

Rolls

  • 5 cups warm water
  • 1 Tbsp salt
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 4 beaten eggs
  • 3 Tbsp or 3 pkg. yeast
  • 12 to 14 cups flour
  • 6 Tbsp vegetable oil
  • Butter, melted (optional)

Orange Roll Variation

  • ½ cup sugar
  • ½ tsp orange extract or 1 tsp grated orange rind
  • ¼ cup softened butter or margarine
  • 1 Tbsp water or orange juice

Directions

Rolls

  1. Combine water, sugar, and yeast in electric mixer bowl. Let yeast rise.
  2. Add oil, salt, and eggs; mix well.
  3. Add flour, mixing between additions. (You have enough flour when sides of mixing bowl are clean.)
  4. Put dough into a greased bowl to rise; cover dough. Let rise until doubled (1 ½ to 2 hours).
  5. Punch risen dough down and divide into 4 sections.
  6. Roll one section out into a circle (¼ inch thick) on a greased counter.
  7. Cut circle into triangles and roll each triangle from wide end to narrow end.
  8. Place shaped roll onto greased cookie sheet and bake at 400°F for 8 to 10 minutes.

Orange Roll Variation

  1. Make dough and roll out the same way.
  2. Mix sugar, orange extract or rind, butter, and water or orange juice.
  3. Spread onto dough before cutting and shaping rolls.
  4. Bake at 400°F for 8 to 10 minutes.
