Mexican Casserole

Ingredients

  • 1⅓ cups fat-free, low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 cup canned chopped green chiles, drained
  • 1 cup chopped onion
  • 1 cup fat-free sour cream
  • ¾ teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon ground cumin
  • ½ teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • 2 (10½ oz) cans condensed fat-free cream of chicken soup
  • 1 clove garlic
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 4 cups shredded cooked chicken breast (about 4 breasts)
  • 2 cups finely shredded sharp cheddar cheese

Directions

  1. Recipe from Roberta.
  2. Preheat oven to 350°F.
  3. In a saucepan, combine chicken broth, green chiles, onion, sour cream, salt, cumin, pepper, cream of chicken soup, and garlic.
  4. Bring to a boil, stirring constantly. Remove from heat.
  5. Spread 1 cup of the soup mixture in a 13×9-inch baking dish coated with cooking spray.
  6. Arrange 4 tortillas over the soup mixture, then top with 1 cup chicken and ½ cup cheese.
  7. Repeat layers, ending with cheese.
  8. Spread remaining soup mixture over the top.
  9. Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes, or until bubbly.
  10. Yields 12 servings.
  11. Enjoy!
