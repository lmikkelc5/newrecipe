Bran Muffins (mom)

Ingredients

  • 1 cup hot water
  • 5 tsp soda
  • 1 cup oil
  • 1 cup honey
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 qt buttermilk
  • 1 cup bran (Nabisco 100% or Mother’s or Miller’s)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ cup wheat germ
  • 5 cups whole wheat flour

Directions

  1. Mix hot water and soda and set aside to cool.
  2. Cream together oil and honey.
  3. Beat in eggs, one at a time.
  4. Add buttermilk, bran, salt, cooled soda water, and wheat germ.
  5. Add flour, one cup at a time.
  6. Bake at 350°F for 20 minutes.

Keep in the fridge covered for up to 6 weeks.
