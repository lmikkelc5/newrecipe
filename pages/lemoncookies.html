Lemon Cookies

Ingredients

Cookies

  • 2 cups butter, room temperature
  • ⅔ cup powdered sugar
  • 1 tsp grated lemon zest
  • ½ tsp vanilla
  • 1 ¾ cups flour
  • 1 ½ cups cornstarch (yes, that much)

Glaze

  • 4 Tbsp butter
  • ¾ tsp grated lemon zest
  • ¼ to ⅓ cup lemon juice*
  • 2 ½ cups powdered sugar

Directions

Cookies

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F.
  2. Beat butter until creamy. Add powdered sugar and mix until light and fluffy.
  3. Add lemon zest and vanilla; beat well.
  4. Add flour and cornstarch and beat until well combined. Do NOT refrigerate.
  5. Roll by hand into 1-inch balls or use a well packed scoop.
  6. Place cookies about 1 inch apart; they do not spread out.
  7. Bake for 15 min on an ungreased cookie sheet until the bottom edges are light brown.
  8. Cool on wire racks before adding the glaze.

Glaze

  1. In a medium bowl combine butter, zest, juice and sugar. Stir well until mixed.
  2. Place a piece of wax paper beneath the wire racks where the cookies have been cooling and drizzle the glaze over them.

*For best results when using lemons, choose the largest lemon you can find and roll it on the counter for about a minute before juicing in order to get as much juice as possible. Zest only the yellow part of the lemon peel; the white portion leaves a bitter taste.

Makes about 5 dozen small, delicate cookies.

From English Trifle by Josi Kilpack.
