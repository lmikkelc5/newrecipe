Easy Black Beans

Ingredients

  • 1 TBSP vegetable oil
  • 1 med. yellow onion, chopped
  • 1 small red pepper, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 15 oz can of black beans, drained
  • ½ c. long grain rice
  • 1 ½ c. chicken broth
  • ¼ tsp. red pepper flakes
  • ¼ tsp. dried thyme
  • 1 bay leaf
  • ½ c. shredded cheese
  • Tortillas

Directions

  1. In a large, heavy sauce pan, heat the oil over medium heat for one minute.
  2. Add the onion and pepper and cook, stirring, for 5 minutes or until the onion is soft.
  3. Add the garlic, beans, rice, chicken broth, red pepper flakes, thyme, and bay leaf and bring to a boil.
  4. Adjust the heat so the mixture bubbles gently. Cover and simmer for 20 minutes or until the rice is tender.
  5. Remove the bay leaf and stir in the cheese.
  6. Roll up in tortillas and serve.
