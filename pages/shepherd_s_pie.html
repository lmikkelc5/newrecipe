Shepherd’s Pie

Ingredients

  • Ground beef
  • Garlic
  • Salt and pepper
  • Onion
  • Seasoning salt
  • Thyme
  • 1 beef bouillon cube
  • 1 cup water
  • ½ can cream of mushroom soup
  • 1 can corn, creamed corn, or frozen mixed vegetables
  • Mashed potatoes (from 6 small–medium potatoes)
  • Shredded cheese

Directions

  1. Fry ground beef seasoned with garlic, salt, pepper, onion, seasoning salt, thyme, etc.
  2. Add beef bouillon cube, water, and cream of mushroom soup to the meat.
  3. Spread vegetables in the bottom of a 9×13 baking dish.
  4. Layer meat mixture over the vegetables.
  5. Top with mashed potatoes.
  6. Sprinkle with cheese.
  7. Bake at 350°F until heated through.
