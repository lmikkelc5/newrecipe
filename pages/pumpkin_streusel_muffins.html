Pumpkin Streusel Muffins

Needs work: The muffin batter steps are missing.

Ingredients

Muffins

  • ¼ cup butter, softened
  • ½ cup sugar
  • ¼ cup packed brown sugar
  • ⅔ cup canned pumpkin
  • ½ cup buttermilk
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 Tbsp molasses
  • 1 tsp orange zest
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp ginger
  • ⅛ tsp cloves
  • ¼ tsp salt

Streusel Topping

  • ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
  • 3 Tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 Tbsp cold butter
  • 2 Tbsp rolled oats (optional)

Directions

  1. For topping, combine the flour and brown sugar and oats (if using) in a small bowl. Cut in butter until the mixture is crumbly.
  2. Spoon streusel topping over the muffins and bake for 20 to 25 minutes or until a tester inserted in the center comes out clean.
  3. Cool in pan for 5 minutes before removing to a wire rack. Enjoy!
