Chicken Enchiladas

Ingredients

  • Soft tortillas
  • Sour cream
  • 1 can cream of chicken soup
  • Chicken (1 whole, or 3 breasts, or 6 thighs)
  • 1 can green chiles
  • 1 cup milk
  • Grated cheese

Directions

  1. Boil the chicken for about 40 minutes, or until fully cooked. Cut into small pieces.
  2. In a bowl, mix together the sour cream, cream of chicken soup, green chiles, and milk.
  3. Add the cooked chicken to the mixture and stir to combine.
  4. Fill tortillas with the chicken mixture and grated cheese, then roll and place in a baking dish.
  5. Pour any remaining mixture over the top and sprinkle with additional cheese.
  6. Bake at 350°F for 30–40 minutes, or until heated through.

Alternative: Layer tortillas, mixture, and cheese repeatedly like a casserole instead of rolling.
