Pasta Salad

Ingredients

Salad

  • Angel hair spaghetti pasta
  • Chicken breasts

Raw Vegetables

  • Carrots
  • Broccoli
  • Tomatoes
  • Olives
  • Radishes
  • Green peppers

Dressing

  • 1 bottle Kraft Zesty Italian dressing
  • ½ to ⅓ jar Salad Supreme seasoning

Directions

  1. Cook angel hair pasta according to package directions. Rinse and cool.
  2. Cook chicken breasts and dice. Set aside.
  3. Place raw vegetables in a large bowl.
  4. Pour in Zesty Italian dressing and stir to combine.
  5. Add cooked pasta and mix well.
  6. Add Salad Supreme seasoning.
  7. Refrigerate, preferably overnight.
  8. Add chicken on the day of serving.
