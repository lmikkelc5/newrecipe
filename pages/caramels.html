Caramels

Needs work: The cooking steps are missing.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups sugar
  • ½ lb butter
  • 1 ½ cups white Karo syrup
  • 1 can Eagle sweetened condensed milk
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Directions

  1. Remove from heat and add 1 tsp vanilla.
  2. Pour into a jelly roll pan with Pam/buttered wax paper lining.

If cooked faster caramels will be lighter colored, if cooked slowly caramels will be darker.
