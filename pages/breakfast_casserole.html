Breakfast Casserole

Ingredients

  • Frozen hash browns (32 oz or ¾ of the bag)
  • Diced ham or diced sausage (cooked)
  • 2 cups grated cheddar cheese
  • 8 eggs
  • 2 cups milk
  • ½ tsp salt
  • Pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Grease or spray a 9x13 pan.
  2. Layer the hash browns, ham or sausage, and cheese in the pan.
  3. In a separate bowl, beat eggs and milk well. Add salt and pepper.
  4. Pour egg mixture over the layers.
  5. Bake at 400°F approx. 40 minutes or until the egg mixture is set and not runny.

Serves 12.

Great for Christmas morning!!
