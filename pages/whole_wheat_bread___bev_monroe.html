Whole Wheat Bread - Bev Monroe

Ingredients

  • 6 cups warm water
  • 2 Tbsp yeast
  • ¼ cup oil
  • ¼ cup lecithin
  • ½ cup honey
  • ½ cup gluten powder
  • 1 Tbsp salt
  • 2 Tbsp dough conditioner
  • 10 cups whole wheat flour

Directions

  1. Pour the warm water (about 105°F) into mixer. Sprinkle the yeast over the warm water.
  2. Using a 1 cup measuring cup, pour ¼ cup oil into the measuring cup first; swirl oil and coat the inside of the measuring cup.
  3. Pour ½ cup honey into the cup on top of the oil—both will reach the ¾ cup line. Pour both into the mixer (honey will not stick to cup).
  4. Next measure ¼ cup lecithin in the same measuring cup so it will also slide out with less sticking.
  5. Now this is the secret of really good bread—set the timer and knead on low speed for at least 10 minutes.
  6. Immediately after kneading, remove dough from mixer and divide into 6 portions.
  7. Spray loaf pans with nonstick spray; shape dough into loaves and put into bread loaf pans.
  8. Let rise until doubled in size, about 40 minutes.
  9. Bake 25 minutes at 350°F. Do not overbake as it dries out bread.

Freeze bread that will not be eaten in one day to keep it moist and fresh.
