Real Simple Hummus

Needs work: The blending step is missing.

Ingredients

  • 1 (15 oz) can chickpeas, rinsed
  • 1 clove garlic
  • ¼ cup olive oil, plus more
  • 2 Tbsp tahini (sesame seed paste; optional)
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • Kosher salt
  • ¼ tsp paprika
  • 2 Tbsp fresh lemon juice

Directions

  1. Add 1 to 2 Tbsp of water as necessary to achieve the desired consistency.

Makes 1.5 cups (¼ cup = 1 serving).
