Baked Caramel Popcorn

Ingredients

  • 1 cup butter or margarine
  • 2 cups brown sugar
  • ½ cup light or dark corn syrup
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 6 qt popped popcorn

Directions

  1. Melt butter, stir in brown sugar, corn syrup & salt.
  2. Bring to a boil, stirring constantly. Boil while stirring for 5 minutes.
  3. Remove from heat, stir in soda & vanilla.
  4. Gradually pour over popped popcorn, mixing well. Turn into 2 large, shallow baking pans.
  5. Bake at 250°F for 1 hour, stirring every 15 minutes.
  6. Remove from the oven. Cool completely.
  7. Break apart & store in tightly-covered containers.

Makes about 5 qt
