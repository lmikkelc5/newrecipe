Florida Keys Chicken Marinade

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp lime juice
  • 1 tsp chopped garlic
  • 4 skinless boneless chicken breast halves

Directions

  1. Blend seasonings.
  2. Place chicken in the mixture.
  3. Marinate 30 minutes or overnight for fuller flavor.
  4. Grill.
