Joan's Broccoli Madness Salad

Ingredients

Salad

  • 1 bunch uncooked broccoli, cut into florets
  • ½ cup crumbled cooked bacon
  • ½ cup cashews
  • ⅓ cup raisins
  • ¼ cup chopped red onion

Dressing

  • 1 cup mayonnaise
  • ⅓ cup sugar
  • 2 Tbsp apple cider vinegar

Directions

  1. Mix the dressing ingredients together.
  2. About 10 minutes before serving, toss the salad with the dressing to allow flavors to meld.
  3. Serve on chilled salad dishes.

6 to 8 servings.

From Souplantation.
