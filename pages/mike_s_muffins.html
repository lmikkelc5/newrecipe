Mike’s Muffins

Ingredients

  • 3 cups flour
  • 1 ½ cups brown sugar
  • ¾ cup butter
  • 1 cup nuts
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp nutmeg
  • 1 tsp ginger
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 tsp baking soda

Directions

  1. Cut butter and add to brown sugar & flour.
  2. Take ¾ cup of mixture and mix with ¼ cup nuts (crumb topping).
  3. Mix dry; mix wet. Combine.
  4. Bake at 350°F, 20-25 minutes.
