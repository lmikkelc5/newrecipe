Café Rio Dinner

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 10 lb chicken breast, boneless/skinless
  • 2 bottles (16 oz) Zesty Italian dressing
  • 2 Tbsp chili powder
  • 2 Tbsp cumin
  • 6 cloves garlic, minced

Pork

  • 1 pork butt/shoulder, or roast
  • 12 oz Coke
  • 2 cans diced green chiles
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1 (16 oz) can tomato sauce
  • Chili powder to taste

Tomatillo Dressing

  • 2 pkgs Hidden Valley Buttermilk Ranch dry dressing mix
  • 2 cups buttermilk
  • 2 cups mayonnaise
  • 2-4 tomatillos
  • 1-2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 bunch cilantro, chopped
  • 1 tsp lime juice
  • 1-2 small jalapeños, seeds removed

Lime Rice

  • 4 Tbsp butter
  • 2 yellow onions, chopped
  • 8 cloves garlic, minced
  • 12 ⅓ cups water
  • 6 cups long grain rice
  • 5 Tbsp chicken bouillon
  • 1 bunch cilantro
  • 4 tsp cumin
  • 4 small cans diced green chiles
  • 2 Tbsp lime juice
  • 1 tsp salt

Directions

Chicken

  1. Mix all ingredients together in a large crockpot. Cook on high for 5-7 hours (about 3-4 hours if using defrosted tenderloins).
  2. Remove chicken and shred with a fork.
  3. Reserve juice and pour over shredded chicken to keep moist. Keep warm.

Pork

  1. Crock pot all ingredients on high for 4 hours.
  2. Shred pork with a fork.

Tomatillo Dressing

  1. Blend ingredients together in a blender. Add additional mayo if dressing is too thin.
  2. Keep refrigerated.

Lime Rice

  1. In a small pan, sauté the onions and garlic in the butter.
  2. In a large pot, boil the water with the bouillon, cilantro, cumin, green chiles, lime juice and salt.
  3. Add the rice to the boiling water; reduce heat and simmer for 30 minutes.
  4. Add onions and garlic.
