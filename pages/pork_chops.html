Pork Chops

Ingredients

  • Pork chops
  • Butter
  • Bread crumbs or crushed crackers
  • Seasonings
  • Milk or water

Directions

  1. Melt butter in the bottom of a 9×13 baking dish.
  2. Mix bread crumbs (or crackers) with butter and seasonings.
  3. Dip pork chops in milk or water, then coat in the crumb mixture.
  4. Place pork chops in the baking dish.
  5. Bake at 425°F for 30 minutes, turning halfway through.
