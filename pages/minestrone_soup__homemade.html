Minestrone Soup (Homemade)

Ingredients

  • ½ cup dried split peas
  • 1 cup dried kidney beans
  • 8 beef bouillon cubes, crumbled
  • 2 tsp dried basil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 tsp dried parsley
  • 1 Tbsp salt
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • 2 cups elbow macaroni
  • 1–2 lb Italian sausage or hamburger
  • Carrots
  • Zucchini
  • 1 onion
  • 4 stalks celery
  • 1 qt canned stewed tomatoes
  • 12 cups water

Directions

  1. Place all ingredients except pasta, vegetables, meat, and tomatoes into a crockpot with 12 cups of water.
  2. Cook on high for 5–6 hours until beans are soft.
  3. Cook meat separately and sauté carrots, zucchini, onion, and celery.
  4. Add cooked meat, vegetables, and stewed tomatoes to the soup.
  5. Cook an additional 1–2 hours.
  6. Add pasta about 30 minutes before serving.

Stovetop option: Cook soup on the stove for about 1 ½ hours, then add vegetables, meat, and tomatoes. Add pasta about 30 minutes before serving.
