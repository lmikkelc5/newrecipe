"Melt in Your Mouth" Chicken Breast

Ingredients

  • 1 cup mayonnaise (can substitute half with Greek yogurt)
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp seasoning salt
  • ½ tsp pepper
  • 2–3 large chicken breasts (slice into smaller pieces or pound thin)
  • Italian seasoned breadcrumbs

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
  2. Place chicken in a large baking dish or on a parchment-lined cookie sheet.
  3. Mix mayonnaise, Parmesan, garlic powder, seasoning salt, and pepper.
  4. Spread mixture evenly over the chicken.
  5. Sprinkle a layer of breadcrumbs over the top.
  6. Bake for 45 minutes, or about 20 minutes for thinner pieces.
