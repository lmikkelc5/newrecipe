Apricot Cake

Ingredients

  • ⅔ cup Wesson oil
  • ⅔ cup apricot nectar
  • 3 tsp lemon extract
  • 4 eggs, separated
  • 1 yellow (or lemon) cake mix
  • 1 ½ cups powdered sugar
  • ⅓ cup lemon juice

Directions

  1. Beat egg whites stiff.
  2. Mix oil, apricot nectar, egg yolks, and lemon extract. Slowly add cake mix. Beat for 5 min.
  3. Fold egg whites into mixture.
  4. Put into ungreased tube or flat pan. Bake for 45-50 min. at 325°F.
  5. While still hot, poke holes in top with a fork.
  6. Mix powdered sugar and lemon juice, then pour over top.

From Grandma Lambert.
