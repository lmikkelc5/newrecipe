Graham Cracker Crust (Marissa)

Ingredients

  • ⅓ cup margarine or butter
  • ¼ cup sugar
  • 1 ¼ cups finely crushed graham crackers (about 18 crackers)

Directions

  1. Melt butter. Stir in sugar.
  2. Add crushed graham crackers. Toss to mix well.
  3. Spread evenly into a 9-inch pie plate. Press onto bottom and sides to form a firm, even crust.
  4. Chill about 1 hour or till firm. (Or bake at 375°F for 4-5 minutes or until the edge is lightly browned. Cool on a wire rack before filling.)
