Bear Creek Minestrone Soup

Ingredients

  • 1 package Bear Creek minestrone soup mix
  • 2 cups extra water
  • 1 tsp bouillon
  • 1 can tomato sauce
  • 1 can black beans
  • Frozen vegetables
  • Costco meatballs

Directions

  1. Make the soup mix, adding the extra water, bouillon, tomato sauce, black beans, frozen vegetables, and meatballs.
