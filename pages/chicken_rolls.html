Chicken Rolls

Ingredients

  • 2 Pillsbury crescent rolls
  • Whole chicken, boiled and shredded (remove skin)
  • Cream cheese
  • Onion salt
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1/2 cube butter
  • Stuffing mix or cracker crumbs

Process

  1. Boil the whole chicken until fully cooked. Remove the skin and shred or break into pieces.
  2. In a bowl, blend the shredded chicken with cream cheese, onion salt, salt, and pepper.
  3. Place the filling into the crescent rolls and roll them closed.
  4. Melt the butter. Roll each chicken roll in the melted butter, then coat with stuffing mix or cracker crumbs.
  5. Bake at 375°F until light brown.
  6. To save for later, freeze after baking and reheat for about 10 minutes.
