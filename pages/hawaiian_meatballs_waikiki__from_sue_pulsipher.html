Hawaiian Meatballs Waikiki (from Sue Pulsipher)

Ingredients

Meatballs

  • 1 lb hamburger
  • Cracker crumbs or oatmeal
  • 1 egg
  • Onion
  • Salt, etc.

Sauce

  • 1 can (13 ½ oz) pineapple tidbits or chunks (drain and save juice)
  • ⅓ cup vinegar
  • 1 Tbsp soy sauce
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • 2 Tbsp cornstarch

Add-ins

  • Green peppers
  • Water chestnuts
  • Carrots

To Serve

  • Rice

Directions

  1. Make meatballs using hamburger, crumbs or oatmeal, egg, onion, salt, etc.
  2. Cook meatballs and let them drain on paper towels.
  3. Drain pineapple and save the juice.
  4. To the pineapple juice, add vinegar, soy sauce, brown sugar, and cornstarch. Mix and stir until smooth.
  5. Pour sauce into a pan. Stir and cook over medium heat until it thickens and boils. Boil for 1 minute.
  6. Add meatballs, pineapple, green peppers, water chestnuts, and carrots.
  7. Serve over rice.
