Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients

  • 2 (4 oz) sticks salted butter
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1 ⅓ cups brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 ½ tsp vanilla (Mexican vanilla)
  • 2 ¾ cups flour (2 ½ if using kamut)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ½ cup semi-sweet chocolate chips
  • ½ cup milk chocolate chips

Directions

  1. Cream together butter & sugar on high for 2 min.
  2. Add eggs and vanilla, mix 1 min or so.
  3. Mix flour, salt, baking soda in separate bowl, then slowly add to wet mixture. DON'T over-MIX! Just barely combine.
  4. Add chocolate chips & fold together.
  5. Bake @ 350°F for 9-10 mins on a tray with parchment paper.
  6. When you pull them out, bang the pan a few times on the counter if you want them flatter (do this while they are still hot).

From Andrea Clawson.
