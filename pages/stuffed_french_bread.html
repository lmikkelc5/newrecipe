Stuffed French Bread

Ingredients

  • 1 loaf French bread
  • 1 lb hamburger
  • ½ onion
  • ½ pepper
  • Celery
  • 1 can Cream of Mushroom soup
  • 1 cup grated cheese
  • 1 can sliced olives

Directions

  1. Slice across top of the bread and pull out the inside (put it in a bowl for later).
  2. Brown hamburger and sauté with onion, pepper, and celery.
  3. Add soup. Mix soup and broken bread.
  4. Add cheese and olives.
  5. Mix meat, bread and soup together, then stuff inside the bread crust.
  6. Put top on and wrap in foil.
  7. Warm through at 350°F for about 20 minutes.

Makes 5 servings.

From Ann Scarle.
