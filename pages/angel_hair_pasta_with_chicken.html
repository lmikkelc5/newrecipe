Angel Hair Pasta with Chicken

Ingredients

  • 4 Tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1-2 chicken breasts, cut in pieces
  • ¾ cup sliced carrots
  • 1 tsp minced garlic
  • 1 bag frozen broccoli florets, thawed
  • ¾ cup chicken broth
  • ½ cup parmesan cheese
  • Angel hair pasta (or spaghetti)

Directions

  1. Heat 2 Tbsp olive oil in skillet over medium-high. Add chicken, stirring until cooked through. Drain on paper towels and keep warm.
  2. Start water boiling for pasta.
  3. Add remaining olive oil to skillet. Cook carrots 3 minutes. Add broccoli and garlic, cook 2 more minutes.
  4. Cook pasta as directed on the package.
  5. Add broth, chicken, and cheese to vegetable mixture. Simmer on low until pasta is ready.
  6. Mix pasta and chicken-vegetable mixture together, sprinkle with additional parmesan cheese and serve immediately.
