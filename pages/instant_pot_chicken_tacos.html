Instant Pot Chicken Tacos

Ingredients

  • 3 boneless skinless chicken breasts (fresh or frozen)
  • 1 oz taco seasoning (1 packet or 2 Tbsp homemade)
  • ⅓ cup chicken broth
  • 1 cup salsa

Directions

  1. Place chicken breasts in the bottom of the Instant Pot. Add chicken broth.
  2. Sprinkle taco seasoning over chicken breasts and top with salsa.
  3. Press “Poultry” (or pressure cook for 15 minutes if frozen, 12 minutes if fresh).
  4. Once done, release pressure and shred chicken with two forks.
  5. Stir shredded chicken into the sauce and serve as desired.

Optional toppings include tomatoes, lettuce, sour cream, and salsa.
