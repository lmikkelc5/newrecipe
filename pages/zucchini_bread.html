Zucchini Bread

Needs work: The steps for adding the zucchini and dry ingredients are missing.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup oil
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 3 tsp vanilla
  • 2 cups shredded raw zucchini
  • 3 cups flour
  • 1 tsp soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3 tsp cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp baking powder
  • ½ cup chopped nuts

Directions

  1. Combine oil, sugar, and eggs in large mixer bowl. Beat well.
  2. Pour batter into 2 loaf pans (8x4x3), well greased and lightly floured.
  3. Bake at 350°F. for about 1 hour, or until the loaves test done. Or use 3 medium loaf pans and reduce baking time to about 30 minutes.
  4. Let stand in pans for ten minutes, then turn out on racks to cool.
