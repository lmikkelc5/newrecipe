Island Treasure Cookies

Ingredients

  • ½ cup and 2 Tbsp flour
  • ¾ tsp baking powder
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • ¾ cup and 2 Tbsp butter, softened
  • ¾ cup brown sugar
  • ⅓ cup sugar
  • ¾ tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 egg
  • ¾ cup toasted coconut
  • ¾ cup chopped macadamia nuts
  • 10 oz milk chocolate chips

Directions

  1. Combine flour, baking powder, salt, and baking soda. Set aside.
  2. In a bowl, combine butter, brown sugar, sugar and vanilla extract. Beat until creamy.
  3. Add egg. Mix well.
  4. Gradually add flour mixture and stir in coconut, macadamia nuts and chocolate chips.
  5. Drop onto ungreased cookie sheets.
  6. Bake 10-12 minutes at 375°F.
  7. Allow to stand 2 minutes before removing.

Makes 2 doz. 3” cookies.
