Ground Turkey and Bell Peppers

Ingredients

  • 3 to 4 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 cup chopped yellow onion
  • Some chopped greens from green onions (optional)
  • 1 bell pepper (red or yellow preferred), chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Kosher salt
  • 1 pound ground turkey, preferably ground turkey thighs
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder (or to taste)
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley or cilantro (or substitute pesto)

Directions

  1. Heat 2 tablespoons olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. Add chopped onion and bell pepper and cook until softened, a couple of minutes.
  3. Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds more.
  4. Push the vegetables to one side of the pan and move that side off the burner.
  5. Add 1–2 tablespoons olive oil to the empty side of the pan.
  6. Add crumbled ground turkey to the hot side, sprinkle with salt and chili powder.
  7. Cook without stirring until browned on one side, then turn to brown the other side.
  8. Once turkey is cooked through, stir in the onions and peppers.
  9. Season with additional salt and chili powder to taste and remove from heat.
  10. Stir in fresh parsley or cilantro.
  11. Serve alone or with steamed rice or noodles.
