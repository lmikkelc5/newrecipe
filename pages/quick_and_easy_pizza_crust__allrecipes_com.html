Quick and Easy Pizza Crust (allrecipes.com)

Ingredients

  • 2 packages or 4 ½ tsp active yeast
  • 2 tsp white sugar
  • 2 cups warm water (110°F)
  • 5 cups bread flour
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • 2 tsp salt
  • Cornmeal, for dusting

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 450°F.
  2. In a medium bowl, dissolve yeast and sugar in warm water. Let stand until creamy, about 10 minutes.
  3. Stir in flour, salt and oil. Beat until smooth. Let rest for 5 minutes.
  4. Turn dough out onto a lightly floured surface and pat or roll into a round.
  5. Transfer crust to a lightly greased pizza pan or baker’s peel dusted with cornmeal.
  6. Spread with desired toppings and bake in preheated oven for 15 to 20 minutes, or until golden brown.
  7. Let baked pizza cool for 5 minutes before serving.
