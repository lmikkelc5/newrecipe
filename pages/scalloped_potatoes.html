Scalloped Potatoes

Ingredients

  • 4 lb potatoes (peeled and sliced thinly)
  • 1 small onion (chopped)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp pepper
  • 3 cups shredded cheddar cheese
  • 3 Tbsp flour
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup chicken broth

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Grease a 9X13 baking pan.
  2. Mix salt, pepper, and flour together—set aside.
  3. Layer ⅓ of sliced potatoes and ½ of chopped onions in the pan.
  4. Sprinkle 1 Tbsp of the flour, salt, pepper mixture.
  5. Top with ⅓ of the cheddar cheese.
  6. Repeat steps 3-5 until all of the potatoes, flour, and cheese are used up.
  7. Combine cream and chicken broth. Pour over top of potatoes.
  8. Bake for 1 hour at 400°F or until potatoes are tender and top is golden.

Layers: Potatoes, onion, salt, cheese. Potatoes, onion, salt, cheese. Potatoes, salt, cheese. Cream.

Makes 12 servings.
