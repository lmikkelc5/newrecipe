Homemade Oreo Cookies

Ingredients

Cookie

  • 2 Duncan Hines Devil’s food cake mixes without the pudding
  • 1 cup vegetable oil
  • 4 eggs

Filling

  • 1 cube margarine
  • 4 cups powdered sugar (enough to make fairly stiff)
  • 1 (8 oz) package cream cheese

Directions

  1. Mix cake mixes, oil, and eggs.
  2. Put cookie dough on a greased cookie sheet in small balls, the size of a walnut.
  3. Bake at 350°F for 8-10 minutes depending on size. Do not over bake.
  4. Mix margarine, powdered sugar, and cream cheese for the filling.
  5. Put filling in between cookies when cool.

They are more moist if they sit overnight.

Makes 40-48 cookies. Freezes well.
