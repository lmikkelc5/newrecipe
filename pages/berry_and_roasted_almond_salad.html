Berry and Roasted Almond Salad

Ingredients

Dressing

  • ¼ cup apple cider vinegar
  • ¼ cup sugar
  • ¼ cup vegetable oil
  • ¼ tsp paprika
  • 1 dash Worcestershire sauce

Salad

  • 2 hearts of romaine
  • Mix of berries - strawberries, blackberries, raspberries, blueberries, etc. (approx. a quart total)
  • Slivered roasted almonds (roast on stove with cinnamon, sugar, salt, egg, and almond extract mixture for about an hour or so, stirring often)

Directions

  1. In a bowl mix the sugar, vinegar, paprika, oil, and Worcestershire sauce. Cover and refrigerate at least 6 hours.
  2. In a bowl, toss berries, romaine, and almonds.
  3. Mix with the dressing just before serving.
