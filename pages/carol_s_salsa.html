Carol’s Salsa

Ingredients

  • 1 (15 oz) can tomato sauce
  • 2 (28 oz) cans tomatoes (or 9 cups fresh chopped)
  • 1 whole onion, chopped (I used a sweet onion or a red onion)
  • 1 green pepper, chopped
  • 1 (4 ½ oz) can green chilis, chopped
  • 2 bunches cilantro (I use only one)
  • 20 drops (more or less) Tabasco… to taste… be careful, depends on how much heat you want
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 tsp cumin

Directions

  1. Mix everything together.
  2. Let sit for hours so the flavors blend.
  3. Serve with chips.
