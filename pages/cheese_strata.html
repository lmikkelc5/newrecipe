Cheese Strata

Ingredients

  • 3 Tbsp butter
  • 3 slices wheat bread & 3 slices white bread
  • 6 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1-1 ½ cups grated cheese
  • 6 slices bacon
  • Green onions

Directions

  1. Melt the butter in the bottom of a 9x13 pan and lay bread inside.
  2. Beat eggs. Add milk and salt and pepper.
  3. Sprinkle cheese on top of bread.
  4. Lay bacon on top of the cheese and add green onions.
  5. Pour eggs over everything, then cover and refrigerate overnight.
  6. Next morning bake at 350°F for 50 minutes.
