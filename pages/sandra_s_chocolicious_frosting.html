Sandra’s Chocolicious Frosting

Ingredients

  • ½ cup butter
  • 2 Tbsp shortening
  • 1 cup baking cocoa
  • 8 oz cream cheese
  • 3 cups powdered sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Directions

  1. Melt butter and shortening.
  2. Whisk in cocoa and stir until smooth.
  3. Let cool until it is cool enough to touch.
  4. Add cream cheese and mix thoroughly.
  5. Add powdered sugar and vanilla. Mix well.

Will cover two 9-inch layers or a 9x13 cake.
