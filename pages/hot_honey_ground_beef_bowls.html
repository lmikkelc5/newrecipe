Hot Honey Ground Beef Bowls

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ lb sweet potato (680 g), peeled and diced into ~¼-inch cubes
  • Salt, pepper, cinnamon (to taste)
  • 1 lb 93/7 lean ground beef
  • 1 Tbsp tomato paste (16 g)
  • 1 Tbsp taco seasoning
  • 2 cups 2% low-fat cottage cheese (452 g)
  • 4 small avocados (400 g)
  • 2 Tbsp hot honey (42 g)

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F and line a half baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Add diced sweet potatoes, lightly spray with cooking spray, and season with salt, pepper, and cinnamon. Bake for 30 minutes, flipping and seasoning again halfway.
  3. While potatoes bake, heat a frying pan over medium-high heat. Add ground beef and cook, breaking it apart, until fully cooked (about 5 minutes). Drain excess liquid.
  4. Add tomato paste, taco seasoning, and a splash of water. Cook for 1–2 minutes, stirring to combine, then remove from heat.
  5. Build bowls with sweet potatoes, taco beef, cottage cheese, and avocado. Drizzle with hot honey and serve.
