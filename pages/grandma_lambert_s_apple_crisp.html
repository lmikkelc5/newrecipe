Grandma Lambert’s Apple Crisp

Ingredients

  • Apples
  • ½ cup flour
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • ⅓ cup brown sugar*
  • ⅓ cup butter

Directions

  1. Sift flour & cinnamon. Stir in brown sugar. Cream in butter.
  2. Fill pie tin with apples and cover with crumb mix.
  3. Bake at 350°F for 1 hour till the apples are tender.

*Or use ½ cup brown sugar and add ¼ cup oats.

With the apples I mix dry pie filling from the Lion House cookbook – halved, or mix in 2-4 Tbsp of sugar and bake for 30 minutes instead.
