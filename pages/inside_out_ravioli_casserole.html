Inside Out Ravioli Casserole

Ingredients

  • 1 lb lean hamburger
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 (10 oz) package frozen chopped spinach
  • 1 (1 lb) can spaghetti sauce with mushrooms
  • 1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
  • 1 (6 oz) can tomato paste
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 (7 oz) package shell macaroni
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • ½ cup soft bread crumbs
  • 2 eggs, well beaten
  • ¼ cup salad oil

Directions

  1. Brown hamburger with onion and garlic.
  2. Cook spinach according to package directions. Drain, reserving liquid.
  3. Add water to reserved spinach liquid to make 1 cup total.
  4. Add spinach liquid, spaghetti sauce, tomato sauce, tomato paste, and salt to the beef mixture. Simmer.
  5. Cook macaroni as usual, then drain.
  6. Combine macaroni with spinach, cheese, bread crumbs, eggs, and oil.
  7. Spread mixture into a 13×9 pan.
  8. Top with meat mixture.
  9. Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes.
  10. Let stand a few minutes before serving.

Note: You may want to use less oil than listed.
