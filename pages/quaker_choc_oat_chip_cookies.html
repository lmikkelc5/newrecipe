Quaker Choc Oat-Chip Cookies

Ingredients

  • 1 cup margarine, softened (½ cup marg., ½ cup shortening)
  • 1 ¼ cups brown sugar
  • ½ cup granulated sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 Tbsp milk
  • 2 tsp vanilla
  • 1 ¾ cups flour (2 – 2 ½ ?)
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 2 ½ cups oats (3 – 3 ¼ ?)
  • 1 (12 oz) pkg chocolate chips
  • 1 cup chopped nuts

Directions

  1. Beat margarine and sugars till creamy.
  2. Add eggs, milk and vanilla; mix.
  3. Add flour and soda, then mix.
  4. Stir in oats, chocolate chips and nuts.
  5. Bake at 375°F. For a chewy cookie cook for 9-10 minutes; 12-13 minutes for a crisp cookie.
