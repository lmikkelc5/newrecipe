Glazed Lemon Loaf

Ingredients

Loaf

  • ⅓ cup melted butter
  • 1 ½ cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ cup nuts
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ cup milk
  • ¼ tsp vanilla

Glaze

  • ¼ cup sugar
  • 2 Tbsp lemon juice

Directions

  1. Blend butter and 1 cup sugar well.
  2. Beat in eggs one at a time. Add vanilla.
  3. Sift the flour and baking powder, and add alternately with the milk to the egg mixture.
  4. Add lemon rind and nuts.
  5. Pour into a loaf pan. Bake at 350°F for 60-70 min.
  6. Mix ¼ cup sugar and 2 Tbsp fresh lemon juice and spoon over the hot loaf immediately as it comes from the oven.
  7. Cool 15 min.

DO NOT CUT FOR 24 HOURS!
