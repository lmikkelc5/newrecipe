Laura's Little Maple Cakes

Needs work: Step 4 (combining the wet and dry ingredients) is missing.

Ingredients

Cakes

  • 1 ¼ cups flour
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • Pinch of salt
  • ½ tsp ginger
  • 2 Tbsp melted butter
  • ½ cup maple syrup
  • 1 egg
  • ½ cup sour cream

Maple Icing

  • 1 cup confectioner's sugar
  • ½ Tbsp softened butter
  • Pinch of salt
  • ¼ tsp vanilla
  • ⅓ cup maple syrup

Directions

Cakes

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
  2. Mix together the flour, baking soda, salt & ginger in a bowl.
  3. Combine melted butter, maple syrup, egg & sour cream in a small bowl.
  4. Lightly grease the cups in a mini-muffin tin.
  5. Fill the mini-muffin cups using two tablespoons, one to scoop the batter and the other to push it off into the tin.
  6. Bake for 12 minutes, or until the cakes are a light golden color.
  7. When the muffins are cool enough to handle, remove from the pans.

Maple Icing

  1. Blend sugar, butter, salt and vanilla.
  2. Add maple syrup and beat until completely smooth.
  3. Decorate each cake by dipping the top of each muffin into the icing and giving it a twist.
