Russian Chicken

Ingredients

  • 2 small bottles Russian salad dressing
  • 1 can cranberry relish (whole berry cranberry sauce, not jelly)
  • 1 package Lipton onion soup mix
  • 4–6 pieces chicken
  • Cooked rice, for serving

Directions

  1. Place chicken in the crock pot.
  2. Combine Russian dressing, cranberry relish, and onion soup mix.
  3. Pour mixture over the chicken.
  4. Cook in the crock pot all day until chicken is tender.
  5. Serve over rice.
