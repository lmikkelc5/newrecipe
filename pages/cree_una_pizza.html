Cree una Pizza

Ingredients

  • 1 Tbsp cornmeal
  • 1 ½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 pkg. (¼ oz or 2 ¼ tsp) quick-rising yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ cup very warm water (125°F-130°F)
  • 1 Tbsp vegetable oil
  • ½ cup pizza sauce
  • 1 ½ cups assorted toppers such as sliced mushrooms, chopped onion, chopped green bell pepper, cooked Italian sausage, or cooked ground beef
  • 2 cups (8 oz) shredded mozzarella cheese
  • ¼ cup (1 oz) grated fresh Parmesan cheese

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Sprinkle baking stone with cornmeal; set aside.
  2. Combine flour, yeast, sugar and salt in medium bowl. Add water and oil; stir until mixture forms a ball.
  3. Place dough on lightly floured surface; knead until smooth and elastic, 3-4 minutes. Cover; let rest 10 minutes.
  4. Place dough in center of prepared stone; roll within 1 inch of edge.
  5. Spread pizza sauce onto crust to within ¼ inch of edge. Top with desired toppings; sprinkle with cheeses.
  6. Bake 16-18 minutes or until crust is golden brown.

Yield: 4 servings.

Nutrients per serving: Calories 420, Total Fat 15 g, Saturated Fat 8 g, Cholesterol 40 mg, Carbohydrate 47 g, Protein 24 g, Sodium 770 mg, Fiber 3 g.
