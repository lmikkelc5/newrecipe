Pumpkin Bundt Cake

Ingredients

Cake

  • 1 can (15 oz, 425g) or 1 ¾ cups solid-packed pumpkin (I highly recommend Libby's)
  • 1 ½ cups (311g) white granulated sugar
  • ½ cup (113g) light brown sugar, packed
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 cup (224g) vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 cups (250g) all-purpose white flour
  • 2 tsp baking soda
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • ¾ tsp ground cloves
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp ground ginger
  • ¼ tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1 tsp pumpkin pie spice

Cream Cheese Frosting

  • 1 package (8 oz) full-fat cream cheese, at room temperature
  • ½ cup unsalted butter, at room temperature
  • 3 cups (342g) powdered sugar
  • ¼ tsp pumpkin pie spice
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • ⅛ tsp salt
  • Chopped (plain or candied) pecans, optional

Directions

Cake

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Generously grease and lightly flour a 10-inch bundt pan. Set aside.
  2. Place the canned pumpkin in a large bowl (if you have wet or fresh pumpkin, make sure to drain off excess liquid) along with the white sugar, brown sugar, eggs, oil, and vanilla extract. Beat with a hand mixer until combined and well blended.
  3. Pour mixture into the prepared bundt pan and bake for 50-65 minutes or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean or with moist cake crumbs. The cake should spring back when lightly touched.
  4. Remove from the oven and cool on a wire rack for 10 minutes before inverting the cake onto the cooling rack to cool completely.

Frosting

  1. In a large bowl, beat ROOM-TEMPERATURE cream cheese and butter until completely smooth. It's important the cream cheese and butter are at the same temperature so you don't get lumpy cream cheese!
  2. Add powdered sugar, pumpkin pie spice, vanilla and salt; beat until completely smooth.
  3. Spread or pipe over the completely cooled cake.
  4. If desired, garnish with chopped candied or plain pecans.
