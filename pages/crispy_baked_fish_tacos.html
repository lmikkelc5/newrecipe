Crispy Baked Fish Tacos

Ingredients

  • 1 cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 2 teaspoons chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon fine sea salt
  • ½ teaspoon freshly-cracked black pepper
  • 1 egg, whisked
  • 1½ pounds firm white fish (cod or halibut), cut into 2-inch pieces

To Make the Fish

Heat oven to 375°F. Spread the panko in an even layer on a baking sheet. Bake for 5–7 minutes, shaking the pan halfway through, until golden brown. Transfer toasted panko to a bowl and reserve the baking sheet.

Add chili powder, garlic powder, cumin, salt, and pepper to the panko and whisk until evenly combined.

Directions

  1. Bake the fish for about 10 minutes, until opaque and flaky. Internal temperature should reach 145°F. Transfer to a wire rack and flake into smaller pieces.
