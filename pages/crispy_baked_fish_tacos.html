Crispy Baked Fish Tacos

Needs work: The steps for coating the fish are missing.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 2 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp fine sea salt
  • ½ tsp freshly-cracked black pepper
  • 1 egg, whisked
  • 1 ½ lb firm white fish (cod or halibut), cut into 2-inch pieces

Directions

  1. Heat oven to 375°F.
  2. Spread the panko in an even layer on a baking sheet. Bake for 5–7 minutes, shaking the pan halfway through, until golden brown. (Keep a close eye on the panko so that it does not burn.)
  3. Transfer toasted panko to a bowl and reserve the baking sheet.
  4. Add chili powder, garlic powder, cumin, salt, and pepper to the panko and whisk until evenly combined.
  5. Bake the fish for about 10 minutes, until opaque and flakes easily with a fork. Internal temperature should reach 145°F.
  6. Transfer to a wire rack and flake into smaller pieces with a fork.

Crispy Panko: To make the panko even crispier, toss it with 1 Tbsp of oil before toasting it in the oven. Let the panko toast until it reaches a pretty deep golden brown for maximum crispiness.

Fish Internal Temperature: The FDA says that the safe internal temperature for cooked fish is 145°F. I usually pull mine out of the oven around 135-140°F, since the temperature will continue to rise a few degrees more while the fish rests.
