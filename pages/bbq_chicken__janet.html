BBQ Chicken (Janet)

Ingredients

  • Chicken, already cooked & cut up
  • ½ cup chopped onion
  • 2 stalks celery, chopped
  • Butter
  • 1 cup ketchup
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • ¼ cup brown sugar
  • 2 Tbsp vinegar
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Brown onion and celery in butter.
  2. Add ketchup, water, Worcestershire sauce, brown sugar, vinegar, salt and pepper.
  3. Bring to boil.
  4. Pour over chicken in a 9x13 Pyrex pan.
  5. Bake uncovered at 350°F (1 ½ hours) until hot/done.
