Blender Wheat Pancakes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup milk (⅓ cup dry milk + 1 cup water)
  • 1 cup uncooked whole wheat
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 Tbsp oil
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 2 Tbsp honey or sugar
  • ½ tsp salt

Directions

  1. Put milk and wheat in blender. Blend on high for 5 mins. until smooth.
  2. Add eggs, oil, baking powder, honey or sugar, and salt. Blend on low.
  3. Bake.
