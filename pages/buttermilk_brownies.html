Buttermilk Brownies

Ingredients

Brownies

  • 4 Tbsp cocoa
  • 2 cups sugar
  • ½ cup margarine
  • ½ cup oil
  • 2 eggs
  • Vanilla
  • 1 cup cold water
  • ½ cup buttermilk
  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda

Frosting

  • ⅓ cup buttermilk
  • ½ cup margarine
  • Approx. 3 ¾ cups powdered sugar or 1 lb (sift)
  • ¼ cup cocoa
  • ½ tsp vanilla
