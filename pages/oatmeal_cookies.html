Oatmeal Cookies

Ingredients

  • ½ cup margarine
  • ¾ cup brown sugar
  • ½ cup white sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • ½ cup flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt (optional)
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp nutmeg
  • 3 cups Quaker oats

Directions

  1. Beat butter and sugars until light and fluffy.
  2. Beat in egg and vanilla.
  3. Combine flour, baking soda, salt, and spices. Add to creamed mixture.
  4. Stir in oats.
  5. Bake on ungreased sheet at 375°F for 8-9 mins.
