Instant Pot Quinoa Burrito Bowls

These Instant Pot Vegan Quinoa Burrito Bowls are an easy and healthy make-ahead meal loaded with fiber and protein.

Recipe Info

  • Prep Time: 5 minutes
  • Cook Time: 20 minutes
  • Total Time: 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 teaspoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 red onion, diced
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 cup quinoa, rinsed well
  • 1 cup prepared salsa
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 1/2 cups cooked black beans (or 1 (15 oz.) can, drained and rinsed)
  • Optional toppings: Avocado, guacamole, fresh cilantro, green onions, salsa, lime wedges, shredded lettuce

Process

  1. Heat the oil in the bottom of the Instant Pot using the Saute setting. Saute the onions and peppers until they start to soften (about 5–8 minutes), then add the cumin and salt and saute another minute. Turn off the Instant Pot.
  2. Add the quinoa and water and stir well, scraping the bottom of the pot to make sure nothing is stuck (to avoid a burn error). Add the salsa and beans on top without stirring again. Secure the lid and set the steam release valve to Sealing.
  3. Press the Rice button (LOW pressure for 12 minutes) or cook at high pressure for 1 minute. Let the pressure naturally release for 15 minutes. When the screen reads L0:15, move the valve to Venting.
  4. When the floating valve drops, remove the lid. Fluff the quinoa with a fork and serve warm with desired toppings.
  5. Store leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge for up to one week. Reheat on the stovetop or enjoy cold.
