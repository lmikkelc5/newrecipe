Chicken Pot Pie

Ingredients

  • ⅓ cup margarine or butter
  • ⅓ cup flour
  • ⅓ cup chopped onion
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp pepper
  • ⅛ tsp thyme
  • 1 ¾ cups chicken broth (14.5 oz can)
  • ⅔ cup milk
  • 2 cups cooked chicken, cut up
  • 1 (10 oz) package frozen peas and carrots (or vegetables of choice)
  • Pie crust

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F.
  2. In a saucepan, melt the margarine or butter over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and cook until softened.
  3. Stir in the flour, salt, pepper, and thyme until well combined and bubbly.
  4. Gradually add the chicken broth and milk, stirring constantly until the mixture thickens.
  5. Stir in the chicken and frozen vegetables.
  6. Pour the mixture into a pie dish lined with pie crust. Top with remaining crust and seal edges.
  7. Bake for 30–35 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the filling is bubbling.
  8. Let cool slightly before serving.
