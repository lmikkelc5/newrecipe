Shelley’s Spaghetti Sauce

Ingredients

  • 2 (16 oz) cans tomato sauce
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 Tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 Tbsp onion
  • ½ tsp oregano
  • ¼ tsp garlic

Directions

  1. Heat olive oil in a saucepan over medium heat.
  2. Add onion and cook briefly.
  3. Stir in tomato sauce, brown sugar, oregano, and garlic.
  4. Simmer until heated through and flavors are combined.
