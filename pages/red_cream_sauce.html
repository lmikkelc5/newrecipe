Red Cream Sauce

Ingredients

  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 medium onion, finely diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 (15 oz) cans tomato sauce
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • Dash of sugar (more to taste)
  • ½ cup half-and-half or cream
  • ½ cup milk
  • Grated Parmesan cheese, to taste
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • 1 ½ lb pasta

Directions

  1. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat.
  2. Add onion and cook until soft.
  3. Add garlic and cook briefly until fragrant.
  4. Add tomato sauce, salt, pepper, sugar, and basil. Simmer.
  5. Stir in half-and-half and milk and heat through.
  6. Serve over cooked pasta and top with Parmesan cheese.
