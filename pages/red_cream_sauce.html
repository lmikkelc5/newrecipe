Red Cream Sauce

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 medium onion, finely diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 (15 oz) cans tomato sauce
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • Dash of sugar (more to taste)
  • ½ cup half-and-half or cream
  • ½ cup milk
  • Grated Parmesan cheese, to taste
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • 1½ lb pasta

Directions

  1. Recipe from The Pioneer Woman, modified by fivelittlepeaches.blogspot.com.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat.
  3. Add onion and cook until soft.
  4. Add garlic and cook briefly until fragrant.
  5. Add tomato sauce, salt, pepper, sugar, and basil. Simmer.
  6. Stir in half-and-half and milk and heat through.
  7. Serve over cooked pasta and top with Parmesan cheese.
