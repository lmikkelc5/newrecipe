Curried Chicken Cabbage Cups

Ingredients

  • 1 chicken breast, seasoned, cooked, and shredded
  • ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 tsp yellow mustard
  • ¼ tsp yellow curry powder
  • ¼ cup unsweetened, dried cherries
  • ⅛ cup crushed cashews
  • Sea salt and pepper
  • 2 purple cabbage leaves

Directions

  1. Mix the cooked, shredded chicken with the Greek yogurt, mustard, curry powder, dried cherries, crushed cashews, sea salt, and pepper.
  2. Spoon the mixture into the purple cabbage leaves to serve as cups.

Serves one (original recipe).
