Cheesecake Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 cup condensed milk
  • ⅓ cup lemon juice
  • 1 (8 oz) package cream cheese (whip it up)
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • Graham cracker crust
  • Fruit filling, any kind

Directions

  1. Mix condensed milk, lemon juice, whipped cream cheese, and vanilla together.
  2. Pour into graham cracker shell.
  3. Top with any kind of fruit filling.

No-bake cheesecake.
