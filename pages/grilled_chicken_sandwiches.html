Grilled Chicken Sandwiches

Ingredients

  • 1½ pounds boneless skinless chicken breasts (about 4 small breasts)
  • 4 hamburger buns or brioche rolls
  • ¼ cup honey mustard sauce or mayonnaise
  • Toppings as desired
  • Marinade
  • 3 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
  • 1 tablespoon brown sugar
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper

Directions

  1. Place the chicken breasts on a cutting board and lightly cover with plastic wrap. Pound to ½-inch thickness.
  2. Combine marinade ingredients and add chicken. Marinate as desired.
  3. Preheat the grill to medium-high heat (375°F).
  4. Remove chicken from the marinade, allowing excess to drip off, and discard marinade.
  5. Grill chicken for 5–6 minutes per side, until no pink remains and internal temperature reaches 165°F.
  6. Brush the cut side of the buns with olive oil and grill cut-side down until lightly toasted.
  7. Spread buns with honey mustard, add toppings if desired, top with chicken, and serve.
  8. Notes
  9. If chicken breasts are large, they can be cut to make 4 portions.
  10. Do not overcook; chicken only needs about 10–12 minutes total.
  11. Chicken breasts may be replaced with boneless skinless chicken thighs.
  12. Homemade honey mustard: Combine ⅓ cup mayonnaise, 1½ tablespoons yellow mustard, 1½ tablespoons honey, and ½ teaspoon garlic powder.
  13. Optional toppings: lettuce, tomatoes, sliced red onion, pickles.
  14. Leftover chicken can be stored up to 4 days in an airtight container.
