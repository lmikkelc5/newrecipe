Au Gratin Potatoes

Ingredients

  • 2 lb potatoes (or 6 medium sized)
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • ¼ cup margarine or butter
  • 1 Tbsp flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp pepper
  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
  • ¼ cup fine dry bread crumbs
  • Paprika

Directions

  1. Cut potatoes into thin slices with skin on, enough to measure 4 cups.
  2. Cook and stir onion in butter in 2 qt sauce pan until tender.
  3. Stir in flour, salt, pepper. Cook over low heat, stirring constantly, until mixture is bubbly. Remove from heat.
  4. Stir in milk and ½ cup cheese. Heat to a boil, stirring constantly. Boil and stir for 1 minute.
  5. Place potatoes in ungreased 1 ½ qt casserole. Pour cheese sauce on potatoes.
  6. Bake uncovered at 325°F for 1 hour 20 minutes or at 375°F for 1 hour.
  7. Mix remaining cheese and bread crumbs. Sprinkle over potatoes. Sprinkle with paprika.
  8. Bake uncovered until top is brown and bubbly (15-20 minutes longer).
