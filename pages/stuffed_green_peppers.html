Stuffed Green Peppers

Ingredients

  • 6 green peppers
  • ½ lb hamburger
  • 1 ½ cups rice
  • 1 can tomato sauce
  • Seasonings: onion, garlic, salt & pepper, oregano, thyme, seasoned salt, etc.
  • Grated cheese

Directions

  1. Cook hamburger. Cook rice.
  2. Mix both with tomato sauce. Add a bit of water as needed to make desired consistency.
  3. Stuff the peppers.
  4. Bake, covered, at 350°F for 30 min.
  5. For the last 5-10 minutes, add grated cheese on top.

Makes 6.
