Honey Garlic Chicken

Ingredients

  • 3 large frozen chicken breasts (if thawed, adjust cooking time)
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 3/4 cup honey
  • 1 TB olive oil
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 1/4 cup sesame seeds (optional)

Directions

  1. Place chicken breasts in a crock pot.
  2. Combine soy sauce, honey, olive oil, onion powder, garlic, salt, and pepper in a bowl and stir until combined.
  3. Pour sauce over chicken.
  4. Cook on low for 4–6 hours, or until chicken easily falls apart.
  5. Serve over cooked quinoa or rice. Spoon extra sauce over the top and sprinkle with sesame seeds if desired.
  6. Enjoy!
