Homemade Caesar Salad Dressing

Ingredients

  • 2 small garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp anchovy paste (see note)
  • 2 Tbsp freshly squeezed lemon juice, from one lemon
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard (I like the brand Maille)
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 cup mayonnaise, best quality such as Hellmann's Real
  • ½ cup freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp freshly ground black pepper

Directions

  1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the garlic, anchovy paste, lemon juice, Dijon mustard and Worcestershire sauce.
  2. Add the mayonnaise, Parmigiano-Reggiano, salt and pepper and whisk until well combined.
  3. Taste and adjust to your liking.

The dressing will keep well in the fridge for about a week.

Note: Anchovy paste can be found near the canned tuna in the supermarket.

Note: I leave out the anchovy paste and it’s ok to substitute with a healthier mayo option, like avocado.

Servings: Makes 1 ⅓ cups (enough for about 10 starter salads). Total time: 10 minutes.

My family loves this rich and creamy Caesar salad dressing. It’s not overly garlicky or fishy — it’s just right.
