French Dip Beef Sandwiches

Ingredients

  • 1 can beef consommé
  • 1 can French onion soup
  • 1 can beef broth (or 1 cup if using bouillon cubes)
  • 1 roast of your choice
  • French rolls

Directions

  1. Add all ingredients to the crock pot.
  2. Cook for a few hours until the roast is tender.
  3. Serve on French rolls.
  4. Serve with the cooking liquid “au jus” for dipping.
